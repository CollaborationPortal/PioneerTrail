Pioneer Trail

Westward in the mid-1840s — a classroom journey of grit, luck, and supplies.

The Journey Begins

In the mid-1840s, people are moving west to territory not yet organized into states. Some seek business opportunities, religious freedom, or a better life. Others chase gold in California, farmland in Oregon, or a new home in Utah.

The trail is difficult, unpredictable, and full of hard work. Keep going until you reach the end!

Gather Your Party

Choose 1–4 pioneers. Each pioneer taps a character to claim it; tap a small picture to change the look. Starting supplies come from the character. The computer acts as banker.
